## Day 4 — Product & Division-Level Profitability Findings

### Product-Level Analysis

**High-profit / high-margin (core products):**
- All 5 Wonka Bar variants — $16.5K–$19.4K profit each, 65–71% margin
- These drive the vast majority of total business profit and volume

**Margin risk — systemic pattern, not isolated:**
- Every non-Chocolate-division product except two negligible-volume items
  (Hair Toffee, Everlasting Gobstopper — both under 20 total units sold)
  sits below the Wonka Bar core margin range (65–71%)
- Kazookles is the most severe case: 7.7% margin (lowest of all 15 products)
  on $1,205.75 sales — clear pricing/cost review candidate
- Lickable Wallpaper is the highest-volume case of this pattern: 50% margin
  on $7,860 sales (2nd-highest non-Wonka product) — not a crisis, but
  meaningfully below the healthy range and worth monitoring

**Low-sales / low-profit (negligible, low priority):**
- Fun Dip, Nerds, SweeTARTS — all under $65 total sales, minimal business impact

### Division-Level Analysis

| Division | Sales | Gross Profit | Gross Margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chocolate | $131,692.90 | $88,824.62 | 67.5% |
| Sugar | $427.48 | $284.73 | 57.7% |
| Other | $9,663.25 | $4,333.45 | 37.7% |

- Chocolate: dominant in both volume and margin efficiency — core business
- Other: lowest margin of all divisions (37.7%) and not statistically
  negligible ($9,663 sales) — driven by Kazookles, Lickable Wallpaper,
  Wonka Gum, Fizzy Lifting Drinks — recommend as primary target for
  pricing/cost structure review
- Sugar: smallest division (n=40, flagged in Day 1 EDA as low sample size)
  but moderate margin (57.7%) — interpret cautiously
  

## Pareto (80/20) Analysis — Findings

- 5 of 15 products (33%) account for 95.1% of total Gross Profit — all 5 are
  Wonka Bar variants
- The remaining 10 products (67% of the product line — all non-chocolate)
  contribute under 5% of total profit combined
- This exceeds a classic 80/20 split — profit concentration is more extreme
  than typical, indicating significant over-dependency risk on a single
  product family (Wonka Bar / Chocolate division)
- Business implication: any disruption to Wonka Bar production, pricing, or
  demand would materially threaten the vast majority of company profit, with
  no meaningful buffer from the rest of the product portfolio.


### Cost vs Sales Scatter — Findings

- The 5 Wonka Bar products form a consistent trend line (~3.3-3.5x Sales-to-
  Cost ratio), confirming their healthy, proportional margin behavior
- Lickable Wallpaper sits visibly below this trend line: at its Cost level
  (~3,900), the healthy-product trend would predict ~13,000-14,000 in Sales,
  but it only reaches ~7,860 — visual confirmation of its comparatively weak
  50% margin
- Low-volume products (Kazookles, Wonka Gum, etc.) cluster too close to the
  origin to visually separate on this scale — their margin issues (e.g.
  Kazookles' 7.7%) are better captured by the Gross Margin metric directly
  than by this scatter plot
- Overall: Lickable Wallpaper and Kazookles are flagged as repricing/cost
  renegotiation candidates; Wonka Bars require no cost structure changes
